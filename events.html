Events & seasons

Use the calendar as inspiration for festivals, cultural trips and seasonal escapes across Great Britain.

Spring

Gardens, countryside walks, Easter breaks and shoulder-season city trips.

Summer

Long daylight hours, outdoor festivals, coast trips and major sporting and cultural events.

Autumn

Woodland colour, food festivals, quieter countryside breaks and city weekends.

Winter

Christmas markets, theatre, museums, festive city breaks and dramatic winter landscapes.

Before you travel

Event dates, tickets and opening arrangements change. Confirm details with the official organiser before booking travel.

Build a weekend

Pair an event with a neighbourhood, museum, restaurant or countryside day to turn it into a complete short break.
