Food & drink

Discover regional dishes, markets, pubs, restaurants and producers as part of the journey.

England

Look beyond the classics to regional cheeses, seafood, bakery traditions, farm shops, diverse city dining and historic pubs.

Scotland

Explore seafood, local produce, traditional dishes, contemporary restaurants and Scotland’s celebrated whisky culture.

Wales

Seek out Welsh cheeses, lamb, seafood, baked specialities, market halls and a growing independent food scene.

Make food part of the itinerary

Visit covered markets, book destination restaurants in advance, stop at independent cafés on road trips and ask local businesses what is seasonal. Opening times and menus change, so check directly before travelling.
