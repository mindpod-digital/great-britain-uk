THE OPEN ROAD

Great British road trips

Slow down, take the scenic route and connect cities, villages, coasts and national parks.

Scottish Highlands

Build a flexible circuit around lochs, glens, mountain landscapes and Highland towns. Allow time for weather and narrow roads.

North Wales

Combine Eryri, the north coast, historic castles and the Llŷn Peninsula over several days.

Cornwall

Link harbour towns, beaches and cliff-top walks, using one or two bases rather than changing accommodation every night.

Lake District & Yorkshire

Pair lake and mountain scenery with the Yorkshire Dales for a varied northern England journey.

South West England

Connect Bath and Bristol with Somerset, Devon and Cornwall for history, countryside and coast.

Practical planning

Check parking, low-emission or clean-air rules, ferry bookings, seasonal road conditions and charging options before departure.
