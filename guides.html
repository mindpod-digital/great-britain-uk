PLAN WELL

Travel guides

Simple, useful planning advice for getting around and making the most of your time.

When to visit

Spring and autumn can offer milder sightseeing conditions; summer brings long daylight hours and peak demand. Winter can suit city breaks and seasonal events.

Getting around

Intercity rail connects many major destinations. Rural areas may need buses, taxis or a car, so plan the final leg before travelling.

How long to stay

A city can work for a weekend, while national parks and multi-stop regions reward slower itineraries of several days.

Weather

Conditions can change quickly, especially in upland and coastal areas. Pack layers and check a reliable forecast before outdoor plans.

Budgeting

Book high-demand transport and accommodation early, compare flexible fares and leave room in the budget for local experiences.

Travel responsibly

Use public transport where practical, respect paths and communities, take litter away and support independent local businesses.

Good to know

Great-Britain.uk covers Great Britain: England, Scotland and Wales. Northern Ireland is part of the United Kingdom but not Great Britain.
